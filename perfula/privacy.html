Privacy Policy

Effective: 22 November 2026 · Perfula Budget (previously Otter Budget)

Perfula Budget is provided by Robel Amirkhanov. This policy covers the app, account and backup services, and these web pages.

Information you enter

The app stores your chosen name, preferences, income, expenses, bills, categories, notes, dates, budgets, savings goals and related records in a local database. We do not connect to your bank, hold money or ask for bank credentials or card details.

Google and Apple sign-in

If you choose to sign in, the provider supplies an identity token that may contain your basic profile and email address. We validate the token and use the provider's unique account identifier to create or find your Perfula account. Our account database stores the provider, that identifier, an internal account identifier and session information; it does not store your Google or Apple password, provider email or profile name. The name you enter in Perfula may be included in your budget backup.

Google data is used to authenticate you, link an identity when you request it, and let you access your own backup. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or calendars. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with advertisers. Infrastructure providers process it only to operate the service; disclosure may also occur if required by law or to protect the service against abuse. Perfula's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Cloud backups

When signed in, the app sends your budget records, settings and onboarding drafts to our account service so you can restore them on another device. Transfers use HTTPS and stored backup payloads are encrypted on the server. This is server-side encryption, not end-to-end encryption: the service holds the key needed to restore your records. Account and backup infrastructure is hosted with Vultr.

On supported iOS versions, users without an active Perfula account may use private iCloud/CloudKit backups associated with their Apple Account. Automatic iCloud sync pauses while a Perfula account is active. Cloud availability depends on connection, storage and provider availability. Conflicting copies may require your choice before replacement.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes payments. RevenueCat helps validate purchases, restore access and manage subscription status. It processes app user identifiers, product and store transaction identifiers, purchase history, status and expiry, and technical context such as platform, app version, locale and currency. We do not send your entered name, budget amounts or transaction notes to RevenueCat. Restoring a store purchase and restoring budget data are separate operations.

Retention and deletion

We keep account identifiers and the current cloud backup while your account exists. Sessions expire and can be revoked. Deleting your cloud account in the app removes its active account record, linked identities, sessions and backup. Your local budget remains on your device; local and iCloud data must be managed separately. Removing the app or deleting your account does not cancel subscriptions. Store and RevenueCat purchase records may remain for subscription operation, fraud prevention and legal obligations.

You can edit or delete local records in the app. For access, correction or deletion requests concerning information we hold, email monoplay.app@gmail.com; we may verify ownership first. You can also remove Perfula's connection in your Google Account, which is separate from deleting the Perfula account. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights and the right to complain to your privacy authority.

Support, security and service providers

We use support messages and contact details to answer requests and retain them as needed for support and legal obligations. Website and infrastructure requests may generate security logs including IP address and browser information. These pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts. The app does not use advertising identifiers to track you across other companies' apps.

Our hosting, email, authentication and purchase providers may process data outside your country. Where a legal basis is required, account and backup processing provides the service you request; support and security serve legitimate operational interests, and legal obligations apply where required. Consent is requested where applicable. No system can guarantee against every loss or unauthorized access.

Provider policies: Google, Apple, RevenueCat, Vultr.

Children and updates

Perfula is a general budgeting tool, not a service directed specifically to children. A parent or guardian should supervise children's use and purchases. Contact us if a child has provided personal information so we can address it. We update this page when practices change and provide additional notice when required.
