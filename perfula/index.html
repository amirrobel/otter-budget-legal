Perfula Budget

A friendly way to record income and expenses, plan monthly category budgets, manage recurring bills and track savings goals.

Perfula is a budgeting app for iPhone and Android. Your entries are financial records, not bank transfers. The main app requires a paid subscription; prices and terms are shown before purchase.

Your budget, across devices

Where account features are available, sign in with Google to save and restore your budget. Sign in with Apple is also available on supported Apple devices. Google sign-in identifies your Perfula account; it does not give Perfula access to your Gmail, contacts or Google Drive.

Account backup and store subscription restoration are separate. Signing in does not purchase a subscription or automatically transfer a purchase between stores.

Support and account deletion

Use account settings in the app to delete your cloud account, or email monoplay.app@gmail.com to request deletion. We verify ownership before acting. Deleting an account does not cancel a store subscription or erase records still stored on your device.
